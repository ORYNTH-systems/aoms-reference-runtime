# AOMS-RG-011 Authority Registry

| ID | Authority Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AOMS-AUTH-001 | Direct | Issued directly by the governing principal |
| AOMS-AUTH-002 | Delegated | Transferred through an authorized delegation |
| AOMS-AUTH-003 | Conditional | Dependent on continuing conditions |
| AOMS-AUTH-004 | Temporal | Limited by time or sequence |
| AOMS-AUTH-005 | Scoped | Limited by action, resource, target, or environment |
| AOMS-AUTH-006 | Collective | Requires multiple participating principals |
| AOMS-AUTH-007 | Emergency | Activated by defined emergency conditions |
| AOMS-AUTH-008 | Revoked | Affirmatively withdrawn |
| AOMS-AUTH-009 | Expired | Outside its validity conditions |
| AOMS-AUTH-010 | Unresolved | Cannot be positively reconstructed |
