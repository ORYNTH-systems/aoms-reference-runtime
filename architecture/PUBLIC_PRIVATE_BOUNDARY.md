AOMS Public and Controlled-Development Boundary
Public Repository

The public repository may contain:

already published doctrine;
released specifications;
executable reference implementations;
public schemas;
public test cases;
reproducible evidence;
public benchmarks;
sourced case studies;
released manuals;
published papers;
standards crosswalks;
citation and release metadata.
Controlled Private Development

The following material must not be automatically placed in the public repository:

unpublished claims architecture;
claim-language alternatives;
undisclosed invention boundaries;
continuation strategy;
unpublished embodiments containing potential new matter;
private counsel analysis;
filing-support analysis;
pre-publication novelty assessments;
confidential implementation information;
unverified allegations concerning reported incidents.
Classification States
PUBLIC-CANONICAL: published and authorized for public release.
PUBLIC-IMPLEMENTATION: public implementation of disclosed architecture.
PUBLIC-RESEARCH: sourced research cleared for publication.
CONTROLLED-DRAFT: incomplete or not authorized for publication.
CONTROLLED-IP: potentially claim-relevant or new matter.
CONTROLLED-REVIEW: awaiting legal, technical, or factual review.
Enforcement Rule

No CONTROLLED-DRAFT, CONTROLLED-IP, or CONTROLLED-REVIEW material may be committed to the public repository without an explicit reclassification record.
