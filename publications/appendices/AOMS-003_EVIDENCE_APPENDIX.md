## Purpose

This appendix binds the AOMS verification methodology to reproducible corpus, fault, replay, distribution, and proof-completeness evidence.

Claims `AOMS-CP-009` through `AOMS-CP-014` cover exact corpus coverage, legacy equivalence, fail-closed exceptions, proof completeness, decision distribution, and replay-resistant execution identity.

## Evidence path

The closure matrix links every claim to a specification, executable component, case, and proof report. The full regression suite includes canonical-corpus, runtime, conformance, and domain-execution tests. Machine reports preserve aggregate outcomes and case-level evidence.

## Interpretation

The verification result applies to the identified reference-runtime version, cases, policies, and evidence. It is reproducible assurance, not a universal proof for every implementation or deployment environment.
