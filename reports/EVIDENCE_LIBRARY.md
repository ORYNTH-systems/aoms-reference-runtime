Evidentiary Scope

The v1 library demonstrates deterministic rejection when one or more modeled execution conditions differ materially from authorized conditions.

It does not yet establish:

balanced classification performance;
false-positive rates;
empirical latency;
four-decision behavior;
six-engine compositional enforcement;
complete transitive provenance;
resistance to injected internal failures.

Those questions are assigned to Phase II.
